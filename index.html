Privacy Policy: TishWish Mallowmoon

Last updated: 11 October 2026
Developer: Dhiraj Mishra
Contact: tishwishco@gmail.com

The short version

TishWish Mallowmoon is a children's play app. It does not collect, store or share personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything the app remembers stays on your device.

What stays on the device

  • Game progress: levels reached and stars earned in each game
  • Friend cards, felt trophies, albums and secret critters found
  • Garden days (the flowers that grow for each day of play)
  • Your child's chosen buddy, outfit and world
  • Settings: sound, music, world sounds and volume, the rest reminder, "Always soft", and "Older child? Open all levels"
  • Whether the premium unlock has been bought (so the app works offline)
  • How often the app was opened (to change the world each time), and how many wrong answers the grown-up question has seen and until when it is locked

This information is never sent anywhere by us. It is not linked to your child's name or identity, because the app never asks for one.

No online backup. Android's automatic backup is switched off for this app, so none of the above is copied to your Google account or to a new phone. A new phone starts with a fresh app, and a grown-up brings the premium unlock back with "Restore purchases" in the grown-ups' area.

What the app does not use

No camera, microphone, contacts, location, accounts, advertising identifiers, analytics, crash-reporting services, or advertising and tracking tools.

Purchases

The app offers one optional one-time purchase (the premium unlock). It is only in the grown-up area, behind a grown-up check (a maths question). Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see payment details. We only receive Google Play's confirmation that the purchase exists. The app uses the network only for buying and restoring a purchase. Google's own privacy policy applies to Google Play.

Children's privacy

The app is made for young children and for families. We do not collect personal information from children or from anyone else. There is no chat, no social feature, no ads, and no link a child can tap that leaves the app. This is in the spirit of children's privacy laws such as COPPA (USA) and GDPR-K (EU/UK).

Permissions

  • com.android.vending.BILLING: lets Google Play process the purchase.
  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: added by Google's Play Billing library so it can talk to Google Play. The app's own code makes no other network requests.

How to delete your data

  • Uninstall the app: this removes everything it saved on the device.
  • Or open Settings > Apps > TishWish Mallowmoon > Storage > Clear data.
  • A grown-up can also use "Reset progress" in the grown-ups' area of the app's Settings (this clears levels, stars, cards, trophies, albums, the garden and outfits; the premium unlock is kept).

We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.
